# Outgoing MTA Proof of Concept Requirements

## Eligibility

All requests evaluate Section A. Chemical, biological, device, and animal-derived selections additionally evaluate Sections B, C, D, and E respectively. Multiple selections evaluate every applicable section.

- Every applicable answer is confirmed true: `eligible`.
- Any applicable answer is false: `ineligible` for the Streamlined Template Process.
- An answer is missing, the category is missing, or the request is outside documented scope: `needs_review`.

The checklist refers to Sections B through F, but no Section F is present.

## Agreement Information

The form collects recipient institution and address, recipient scientist, provider scientist, material description, research purpose, duration, shipping-cost payer, and whether the material is provided at no cost. Missing agreement information is reported separately and never changes eligibility.

## Template Selection

Template selection always requires review in this version. The supplied documents do not define when to select among AUTM Biological, AUTM Chemical, the Simple Letter Agreement, and the UBMTA Implementing Letter. The app reports document-supported candidates but never selects one automatically.

## Scope

This local application is not connected to eRPM, does not provide legal approval, does not automate signatures, and does not generate agreements. Use synthetic data only.
